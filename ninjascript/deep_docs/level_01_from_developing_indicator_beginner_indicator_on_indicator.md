# Beginner - Indicator on Indicator
## [Indicator on Indicator Overview](https://developer.ninjatrader.com/docs/desktop/beginner_indicator_on_indicator#indicator-on-indicator-overview)
In this beginner level tutorial we are going to build a custom indicator that calculates a moving average of volume. This indicator will show you how to use the built in indicators of [Moving Average (SMA)](https://developer.ninjatrader.com/docs/desktop/moving_average_simple_sma) and [Volume](https://developer.ninjatrader.com/docs/desktop/volume).
